192.168.1.1 Admin Login & Setup Guide

192.168.1.1 is the world's most widely assigned default gateway IP, managing home and small business local area networks.

RFC 1918 Private IPv4 Reference
Gateway IP: 192.168.1.1 • Subnet: 255.255.255.0 (/24)
Default Credentials: admin / admin or admin / password • Usable Hosts: 192.168.1.2 - 192.168.1.254

Why is 192.168.1.1 Not Loading?

  • Typo check: Ensure you are typing numbers, not letters (avoid "192.168.l.l" with lowercase L).
  • VPN Active: Disconnect any active VPNs (Work VPN, NordVPN, ExpressVPN) as they route DNS away from your local subnet.
  • Protocol: Use http://192.168.1.1 rather than https:// unless your router enforces SSL certificates.
  • Different Gateway: Your router might use a different subnet. Use our Local Gateway Auto-Detector to confirm your exact IP.
