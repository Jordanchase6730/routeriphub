How to Factory Reset DrayTek Vigor 2135 Series

Hardware Reset Instructions:

While router is running, press and hold the Factory Reset pinhole on the front panel for 10 seconds until the ACT LED starts blinking rapidly. Release and wait 1 minute.

LED Indicator Diagnostic Cheat-Sheet

LED Name Normal State Error / Alert State Description & Function
ACT Blinking Green (1 sec cadence normal) Solid or Off (Firmware hung/system error) DrayOS activity.
WAN 1 / WAN 2 Solid Green Blinking (Negotiating) / Off Physical broadband uplink.
VPN Solid Green (Active tunnel) Off IPsec/SSL VPN status.
